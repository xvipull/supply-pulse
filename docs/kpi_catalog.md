# KPI catalog

| KPI | Definition | Target / interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| OTIF | % of PO lines received on or before promised date and at ordered quantity | ≥92% target |
| Fill rate | Received quantity ÷ ordered quantity | Delivery completeness |
| PPV | Received quantity × (invoice unit price - contract unit price) | Positive = adverse variance |
| Contract compliance | % of records marked compliant with active contract controls | Higher is better |
| Defect rate | Defect quantity ÷ received quantity | Quality signal |
| Risk priority | Weighted blend of OTIF, fill, defects, compliance and adverse PPV | Triage only; not a supplier decision |
| Three-way match | % of records with price match and full receipt | Reconciliation control |
