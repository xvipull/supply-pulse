# Assumptions & limitations

- Every synthetic PO represents one reportable matchable line.
- A receipt on the promised date is on time; receipt quantity must meet ordered quantity to be in full.
- Invoice prices are in USD and PPV is assessed against the contract unit price.
- Risk-score weights are transparent prototype weights, requiring stakeholder calibration before production.
- The data refresh date shown in the dashboard is a demo snapshot rather than a live operational feed.
